How it works

Fifteen minutes to start. Then it learns on its own.
01
We get to know them

A 15-minute chat with you or with them: what they used to love, who they still see, and what’s hard now (stairs, evenings, distance).

02
You get a short list

Five or six real things near their door, with the details a listing leaves out: cost, steps, time of day, whether anyone will say hello.

03
It learns

Tell us what they went to and what they skipped; a reply or a tap is enough. By the third month the list fits the person, not the city.
